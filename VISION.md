# Vision

**Purpose:** a secure, reliable way to talk to an AI assistant about your YNAB plan and make the changes you direct.

**Who it is for:** people who budget with YNAB and want to ask questions and give instructions in plain language, from a desktop or a phone; plus self-hosters and outside contributors.

**In 1-2 years:** the trusted, standard open-source YNAB connector, listed in the major MCP directories, running locally or as a hosted Worker with any MCP client.

**Guiding principles:**
1. Your money is safe by default: read-only unless you opt in, tokens never reach the model, and nothing is written except what you ask for.
2. Directed, not autonomous: it acts on your instructions; running the plan on its own belongs to YNAB Auto Plan.
3. Provider-neutral: no lock-in to one AI vendor or client.
4. A few strong tools beat many overlapping ones.
5. Security fixes ship fast, followed by a release.
6. Outside contributors get a clear, respectful path to land changes.

**Non-goals:** replacing the YNAB app, other budgeting services, a hosted multi-user service for strangers, autonomous budgeting.
